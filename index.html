Is patient data safe with SenseBeat?

SenseBeat is built and supported by MYSense Sdn Bhd, a Malaysian-registered company compliant with PDPA 2010 and the 2024 amendments. We handle marketing lead data only (name, contact, enquiry source), never clinical records or EMR data, and we provide a signed Data Processing Agreement on request.

Will my team actually use it?

There's nothing for them to build. Our team sets everything up, then runs a hands-on training session with your staff. Day to day, it's a 10-minute morning check of one dashboard, and support is one tap away on the in-app support button.

We already use spreadsheets or another system.

SenseBeat sits on top of what you have. It connects to most tools via webhook and Zapier, and it doesn't replace your clinic management system. Bring your current costs to the demo: most businesses find they pay more across separate tools than SenseBeat costs.

How fast can we go live?

7 to 14 business days from kickoff. You send your logo, WhatsApp Business account and ad account access on day 1; we build, test, train your team and hand over.

Is the messaging KKLIU-compliant?

Yes. Every automated template for healthcare clients is reviewed against KKLIU advertising guidelines before deployment, and broadcasts use explicit opt-in consent flows, so they're MMC-safe by default.

Does it fit my specialty?

SenseBeat ships with pre-configured flows for dental, aesthetic, GP, chiropractic, wellness and specialist clinics, each with the five-stage clinic journey ready on day one. Other businesses run the same system with a pipeline mapped to their own sales steps.
