Is patient data safe with SenseBeat?

SenseBeat is operated by MYSense Sdn Bhd, a Malaysian-registered company compliant with PDPA 2010 and the 2024 amendments. We handle marketing lead data only — name, contact, enquiry source — never clinical records or EMR data, and we provide a signed Data Processing Agreement on request.

Will my team actually use it?

There's nothing for them to build. MYSense sets everything up in 7 days, then walks your front desk through it live — a 30-minute recorded session. Day to day, it's a 10-minute morning check of one dashboard, and your account manager is one WhatsApp message away.

We already use spreadsheets or another system.

SenseBeat sits on top of what you have — it connects to most tools via webhook and Zapier, and we don't replace your clinic management system. Bring your current costs to the demo: most clinics find they're paying more across separate tools than SenseBeat costs all-in.

How fast can we go live?

Seven working days from kickoff. You provide your logo, WhatsApp Business account and ad account access on day 1; we build, test and hand over by day 7 — or your setup fee is fully refunded.

Is the messaging KKLIU-compliant?

Yes. Every automated template for healthcare clients is reviewed against KKLIU advertising guidelines before deployment, and broadcasts use explicit opt-in consent flows — MMC-safe by default.

Does it fit my specialty?

SenseBeat ships with pre-configured flows for dental, aesthetic, GP, chiropractic, wellness and specialist clinics — each with the five-stage clinic journey ready on day one. Non-healthcare businesses run the same system with a pipeline mapped to their own sales steps.
